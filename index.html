Measures the real end-effector pose with an NDI Polaris optical tracker through tf2 and reports average and maximum translational and rotational error.

C++ ROS 2 MoveIt KDL Eigen tf2 Optical Tracking

Collision-Aware Motion Planning

Applied Robotics Lab

ROS 2 C++ node that loads an STL obstacle course into the MoveIt planning scene as a collision object, plans a collision-free trajectory for a Mitsubishi RV-3S industrial arm, and previews it in RViz. The plan only executes on a game-controller button press, using rising-edge detection so one press triggers exactly one motion.

C++ ROS 2 MoveIt Motion Planning Collision Avoidance RViz
